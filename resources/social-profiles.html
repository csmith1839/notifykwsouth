Set up your business social profiles

Business profiles — not your personal pages — are what SocialBAY and Command post to, what clients vet you with, and what keeps your advertising compliant. Set them up once, properly.

  1. Facebook Business Page. From your personal profile: Menu → Pages → Create. Category "Real Estate Agent". A personal profile can't be connected to posting tools — the Page is what everything plugs into.
  2. Instagram professional account. Settings → Account type → switch to Professional (Business), then link it to your new Facebook Page. This link is what lets tools post to both at once.
  3. LinkedIn. Update your headline and experience to your role here; recruiters, vendors, and referral partners all look.
  4. Google Business Profile. Free at business.google.com — it's what shows when someone Googles your name, and where reviews live. Start collecting reviews from closing #1.
  5. Brand them all the same way: the same headshot everywhere, official logos from go.kw.com, and follow the KW style guide. On every business profile, include Keller Williams Indy Metro Partners by name — Indiana advertising rules require your brokerage to be identifiable, and it protects you.
  6. Now connect them: plug your pages into SocialBAY (the office pays for it) and add the links to your Marketing Profile.
