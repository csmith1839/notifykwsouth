The two KW apps — and which one does what

There are two apps, and mixing them up is a classic week-one move. One runs your business; the other is what your clients use.

Command App — yours

Your database in your pocket: look up and add contacts on the go, import your phone book, log calls and notes right after a conversation, manage tasks and Opportunities, and get notified when a lead comes in.

App Store · Google Play · official guide

KW App — your clients'

The consumer home-search app, branded to you. Every agent gets a personal download link — clients who search with your app are searching with you, and their activity feeds your Command. Share your link in your email signature and with every new buyer.

Get your link — official guide

  1. Install the Command App and log in with your KW credentials.
  2. Import your phone contacts — the two-minute job that builds your database.
  3. Turn on notifications so a new lead never waits until you're back at a desk.
  4. Grab your KW App link from Command and add it to your email signature today.
