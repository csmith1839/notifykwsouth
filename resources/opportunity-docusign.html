Office SOP

Opportunity & DocuSign workflow

The full flow from a new client to submitted compliance documents. Each step has a matching how-to on answers.kw.com.

  1. Create the contact in Command.
  2. Create the Opportunity from the contact record.
  3. Complete the compliance checklist for your side of the deal.
  4. Start the transaction in the Opportunity.
  5. Prepare your documents for signing.
  6. Create the DocuSign envelope and route for signatures.
  7. Upload the signed documents back to the Opportunity.
  8. Submit for review. Compliance reviews and you're clear to close.
