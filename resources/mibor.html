How to use MIBOR & the BLC

Most of our agents are MIBOR members — this is your MLS and it comes with far more than listing search. Here's where everything lives.

  1. Log in at member.mibor.com — that's MIBOR Central. One login; every BLC tool below is launched from inside it. Bookmark it and put it on your phone's home screen.
  2. connectBLC is your daily driver. The BLC® Listing Service platform: search listings, run hotsheets, set up client auto-emails and portals, and add/edit your own listings. If you only learn one tool deeply, learn this one.
  3. Pull ownership & tax data with CRS Data. Statewide public records for all 92 Indiana counties — owner names, tax bills, sales history, mortgage info. This is where FSBO and farming research starts.
  4. Showings & lockboxes. MIBOR pairs a digital showing scheduler with electronic lockbox access (Supra One scheduling, MasterLock Bluetooth boxes). Office-specific details are on Showings & lockboxes.
  5. Turn on FOREWARN — the safety tool. Do not skip this one. Free with your MIBOR membership: before you meet someone you've never met, FOREWARN verifies who they are from just their phone number — identity, criminal history, address history — in seconds, from the app. Download the FOREWARN app and register with your MIBOR membership: forewarn.com. Meeting strangers is the job; walking in blind doesn't have to be.
  6. Know the rest of the toolbox — your dues already pay for it (full list): Domus Analytics market dashboards for listing presentations, Down Payment Resource for buyer assistance programs, CubiCasa floor plans for your listings, RealReports AI property reports, Property Shield fraud monitoring, and Gitcha, the buyer-side marketplace.
  7. Get trained by MIBOR itself. MIBOR runs free classes and webinars on all of these — check mibor.com under events, and watch Market Center News for sessions we host.
Not a MIBOR member? The office also belongs to the Terre Haute board (THAAR) and the Bloomington board (IRMLS) — if your market is there, you'll use that board's MLS instead, and the office can point you to its equivalent tools. Joining details: License & MLS.

SOP last reviewed: August 2026. Tool lineup per MIBOR's official BLC technology page — it evolves, so trust mibor.com over this page if they disagree.
