How to use MIBOR & the BLC

Most of our agents are MIBOR members — and most only ever use the listing search. Your dues buy a whole business toolkit. This is the tour, grouped by what you're trying to do.

First: get in

  1. Log in at member.mibor.com — that's MIBOR Central. One login; every BLC tool below launches from inside it. Bookmark it and add it to your phone's home screen.
  2. Turn on FOREWARN before anything else. The free safety tool — verify who you're meeting from just a phone number. Full setup: the FOREWARN SOP.

connectBLC — the daily driver

The BLC® Listing Service platform. If you only learn one tool deeply, learn this one — it's where deals start.

  1. Master search first. Build searches by area, price, and criteria; save the ones you run weekly so they're one click next time.
  2. Run the hotsheet every morning. New listings, price changes, pendings, and solds in your market since yesterday — five minutes that makes you the agent who knows. Pair it with your coffee and your daily conversations.
  3. Put your buyers on auto-email. Saved search + client portal = your buyer sees matching homes the moment they list, from you, not from Zillow. This is the single highest-leverage feature in the system.
  4. Pull comps for pricing. Sold data straight from the source — the backbone of every CMA and listing presentation you'll build.
  5. Add and edit your listings. Enter the listing, upload photos and docs, and keep statuses current — status deadlines are board rules with fines behind them. Remember the office side too: the 48-hour paperwork checklist.

Research like an appraiser

  1. CRS Data — public records for all 92 Indiana counties. Owner names, tax bills, sales history, mortgage data. Use it to prep FSBO conversations, research your farm neighborhood, and answer "what did they pay for it?" in seconds.
  2. RealReports — an AI-assembled dossier on any property. Permits, hazards, liens, zoning — the questions a buyer will ask, answered before the showing.
  3. Domus Analytics — market stats that win listings. Interactive dashboards for your county or neighborhood: months of inventory, price trends, days on market. Drop real charts into your listing presentation instead of "the market's hot."

Win more business with the extras

  1. Down Payment Resource — matches buyers to assistance programs they qualify for. The answer to "we don't have enough saved" that turns a dead lead into a closing.
  2. CubiCasa — scan a home with your phone, get a professional floor plan for your listing. Listings with floor plans get more attention; this makes them free.
  3. Gitcha — the Buyer Listing Service. Post what your buyer is looking for and let inventory come to you; watch it for matches to your sellers too.
  4. Property Shield — monitors for fraudulent copies of your listings on the internet and helps take them down. Set-and-forget protection for your sellers.

Showings, lockboxes & logistics

  1. Schedule and manage showings through MIBOR's showing tools (Supra One scheduling, MasterLock Bluetooth lockboxes). The office specifics live on Showings & lockboxes.

Stay sharp — and licensed

  1. Take MIBOR's free classes. Live trainings and webinars on every tool above — the MIBOR events calendar has the schedule, and Market Center News flags the ones we host.
  2. Knock out your CE for free. Your membership includes 12 hours of state-approved continuing education every year — details, deadlines, and the 30-hour post-licensing rule for new brokers: License renewal & CE SOP.
Not a MIBOR member? The office also belongs to the Terre Haute board (THAAR) and the Bloomington board (IRMLS) — if your market is there, you'll use that board's MLS instead, and the office can point you to its equivalent tools. Joining details: License & MLS.
