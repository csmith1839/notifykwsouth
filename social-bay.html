SocialBAY — social media on autopilot

The office pays for a SocialBAY subscription (by Back At You) for our agents: it posts listings, market updates, and engagement content to your social pages automatically, so you stay visible while you're out actually selling.

  1. Set up your business pages first. SocialBAY posts to a Facebook Business Page and Instagram professional account — not personal profiles. Do the business profiles SOP before anything else.
  2. Get your invite from the office. Your subscription is provisioned through us — reach out to Marissa (marissacorey@kwe.com) or the front desk and she'll get your invite sent.
  3. Connect your pages inside SocialBAY: Facebook Page, Instagram, LinkedIn. Their starter guide walks every connection with screenshots.
  4. Pick your content settings — market area, tone, how often it posts. Then let it run; you can review or swap any scheduled post.
  5. Learn as you go with their how-to videos: Back At You's tutorial playlist on YouTube — short, task-by-task walkthroughs.
Also in your toolkit: Command can post social Designs straight to your connected Facebook Page — guide: Connect Your Facebook Account for Social Posts. SocialBAY is the autopilot; Command posts are your hand-flown one-offs.

SOP last reviewed: August 2026. Automation keeps you visible — it doesn't replace conversations.
