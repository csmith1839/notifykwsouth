Momentum Monday — your weekly business-building appointment

Consistency is what builds a real estate business, and Momentum Monday is where it happens. In one structured session you knock out three of your five primary job responsibilities for the week:

  1. Practice Conversations — build confidence in client interactions before they're live.
  2. Lead Generation & Follow-Up — fill and nurture your pipeline, right there in the room.
  3. Market Research & Strategy Planning — increase your knowledge and credibility, and set the week's priorities.
