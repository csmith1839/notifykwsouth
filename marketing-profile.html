Complete your Marketing Profile

Ten minutes here powers everything: every Design, email, social template, and your agent site pull from this one profile. Do it before you touch any marketing tool — or your flyers go out with blanks.

  1. In Command, click your profile icon (top right) → Settings.
  2. Under Command Settings, open Marketing Profile.
  3. Add your headshot — the same one everywhere builds recognition. Need one? $50 with Autumn.
  4. Fill every field: display name, phone, email, your license number, job title, and your social links (set those up first: business profiles SOP).
  5. Save, then test it: open Designs and auto-create a template — your info should flow in by itself. Guide: Get Started with Designs.

Official walkthrough: Complete Your Marketing Profile. SOP last reviewed: August 2026.
