Set your pace

The five steps above are the path — everything else here (training, events, tools) flexes around them. And agents start with very different amounts of time: some with 40 hours a week, some building Double Duty around a job or a family with 5 or 10. You may see more here than you can reasonably attend. That's okay. The goal is not to attend everything. The goal is to consistently execute the right activities for the business you're building — and that's exactly what your first coaching call is for.
