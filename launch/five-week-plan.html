Your first 5 weeks

The official new-agent checklist, rebuilt around one idea: you're here to build a business, not finish paperwork. Every week, the business-building work comes first; the housekeeping is labeled as housekeeping and meant to be knocked out fast.

How to use this plan: items marked Essential are the business itself — do those even in a five-hour week. Everything else is here for when you have the capacity, and your coach helps you choose what fits. Building Double Duty around a job or family? These five weeks stretch to your timeline — the order matters more than the speed. And if you notice your real-estate time going to getting ready to do real estate, just come back to the activities that create opportunities: people, conversations, and appointments.
