The Launch Plan

Eight weeks, one destination: your first closed deal. Weeks 1–5 build the engine — database, habits, skills. Weeks 6–8 drive it to a payday. Business-building first; housekeeping labeled as housekeeping.

How to use this plan: items marked Essential sit at the top of every week — they're the business itself, so do those even in a five-hour week. Everything else is here for when you have the capacity, and your coach helps you choose what fits. Building Double Duty around a job or family? These weeks stretch to your timeline — the order matters more than the speed. And if you notice your real-estate time going to getting ready to do real estate, just come back to the activities that create opportunities: people, conversations, and appointments.

Weeks 1–5 complete? That's the launch milestone. You're ahead of the average new agent — and if you're on the supported model, your company-dollar split changes in your favor: call (317) 882-5900 to confirm your new split. Now don't stop — the payday phase starts below.
Weeks 1–5 built the engine. Now drive it down the funnel — both sides of it. Buyer side: conversations → buyer appointments → signed Buyer Exclusive Agency agreements. Seller side: conversations → listing appointments → signed listing agreements. Agreements become pended deals; pendings become closings; closings become paydays. None of it runs on a fixed calendar — clients move when clients move — so weeks 6–8 are the pattern, not a deadline. The one thing you control is the top of the funnel: keep the conversations and appointments flowing and the rest becomes inevitable. (Supported-model agents: finishing weeks 1–5 is what changes your split — the milestone above… then keep going.)
