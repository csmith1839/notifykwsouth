Canva Pro & Gemini — already yours

Your @kw.com Google account quietly includes two premium tools most agents pay for: Canva's enterprise plan loaded with KW templates, and Google's Gemini AI. Claim both in five minutes.

Canva Pro (KW enterprise)

  1. Go to canva.com → Log in → Continue with Google and pick your @kw.com address. That lands you in KW's enterprise workspace — Pro features, no charge. Official guide: Get Started with Your KW Canva Account.
  2. Use the KW brand templates — pre-built, on-brand designs for listings, open houses, and social: how to find them.
  3. Let Canva pull your KW data: your listings and profile can flow into designs automatically — connect it here. (Fill your Marketing Profile first.)
  4. On your phone, log in to the Canva app the same way: app login guide.

Gemini (Google's AI)

  1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with your @kw.com account. Official guide: Access Google Gemini with your @kw.com account.
  2. Put it to work on drafts: listing descriptions, email replies, social captions, event ideas — then edit so it sounds like you.
  3. Two rules: verify anything factual before it reaches a client (numbers, law, dates), and never paste in a client's private details. AI drafts; you're the licensee.
